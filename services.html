Our Comprehensive Services

Delivering engineering excellence across utility infrastructure, power systems, facility management, and critical supply operations to support national energy reliability.

Building Management

Facility Operations
  • Mechanical & Electrical (M&E): Comprehensive maintenance and operational management.
  • Electrical Systems: Diagnostics, regular inspection, and scheduled servicing.
  • Air Conditioning Systems: Facility climate and environmental airflow regulation.
  • Building Renovation & Maintenance: General structural upgrades, repairs, and refurbishment.
  • Cleaning & Area Maintenance: Commercial facility cleaning and landscape preservation services.

Mechanical Systems

Engineering & HVAC
  • Air Conditioning & Ventilation: Professional installation and maintenance for split air units, commercial chillers, Air Handling Units (AHUs), and cooling towers.
  • Pumping Systems: Full installation, repairs, and routine servicing of commercial and industrial water pump stations.
  • Control Interfaces: Switchboard panel servicing, mechanical component overhauls, and distribution upkeep.

Electrical & Solar Systems

Core Expertise
  • Low Voltage (LV) Systems: Scheduled installation and maintenance of electrical wiring, public address (P.A.) systems, and communications.
  • Infrastructure & Lines: Cabling work, electrical equipment installation, municipal street lighting, and overhead distribution lines.
  • Standby Generator Systems: Scheduled servicing, cabling, and Automatic Main Failure Board (A.M.F.B) panels.
  • Solar Photovoltaic (PV): Provision, assembly, testing, and commissioning of on-grid and off-grid solar systems.
