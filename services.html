Mechanical Systems

Engineering & HVAC
  • Air Conditioning & Ventilation: Installation and maintenance of air conditioning and ventilation systems, including work on split air conditioners, chillers, AHUs, and cooling towers.
  • Pumping Systems: Installation and maintenance of pumping systems, including work on switchboard panels and mechanical components.

Electrical & Solar Systems

Core Expertise
  • Low Voltage (LV) Systems: Scheduled installation and maintenance of low-voltage electrical systems such as wiring, P.A. systems, and telephone systems.
  • Infrastructure & Lines: Scheduled installation and maintenance of low-voltage electrical systems including cabling work, electrical equipment, street lighting, and overhead lines.
  • Standby Generator Systems: Scheduled installation and maintenance of standby generator systems, including Automatic Main Failure Board (A.M.F.B) panels and associated cabling work.
  • Solar Photovoltaic (PV): Installation and provision of solar systems.
