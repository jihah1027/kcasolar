About Us

Kejuruteraan Cahaya Abadi Sdn. Bhd. (KCA) is an engineering company registered and operating in Malaysia, with expertise in electrical substation works, high and low voltage power systems, and utility infrastructure maintenance, particularly for projects under Tenaga Nasional Berhad (TNB).

Since its establishment, KCA has remained committed to delivering high-quality and safe technical services that meet industry standards.

"We believe that every project is not merely a technical task, but a responsibility entrusted to us to safeguard the continuity of the nation’s energy supply."

About Our Managing Director

HISHAM MOKHTAR is the Managing Director of Kejuruteraan Cahaya Abadi Sdn. Bhd., with nearly 30 years of experience in the electrical engineering industry. He has extensive expertise in installation, maintenance, and development of electrical systems, particularly in substation projects and energy infrastructure that require advanced technical skills and strict adherence to safety standards.

Under his leadership, Kejuruteraan Cahaya Abadi Sdn. Bhd. has grown into a company that emphasizes professionalism, quality workmanship, and full compliance with safe work procedures, especially in projects involving Tenaga Nasional Berhad (TNB). His strategic approach and meticulous focus on ensuring efficient operations and a disciplined work culture have been the foundation of the company's strong reputation in the industry.

TVET Talent Development

KCA plays an active role in developing young national talent by providing structured industrial training specifically designed for TVET graduates. This initiative delivers technical students genuine hands-on exposure at active project sites, instilling rigorous safe work procedures and energy industry engineering standards.

Guided directly by seasoned engineering personnel, this program fosters competent, job-ready technicians equipped to meet high-demand industry environments.

Strategic Partnership: In collaboration with Kolej Vokasional Seri Manjung under the Ministry of Education Malaysia.
