Who We Are

Safeguarding the continuity of the nation's energy supply through engineering excellence.

About Us

Kejuruteraan Cahaya Abadi Sdn. Bhd. (KCA) is an engineering company registered and operating in Malaysia, with expertise in electrical substation works, high and low voltage power systems, and utility infrastructure maintenance, particularly for projects under Tenaga Nasional Berhad (TNB).

Since its establishment, KCA has remained committed to delivering high-quality and safe technical services that meet industry standards.

"We believe that every project is not merely a technical task, but a responsibility entrusted to us to safeguard the continuity of the nation’s energy supply."

About Our Managing Director

HISHAM MOKHTAR With nearly 30 years of experience in the electrical engineering industry, he has extensive expertise in installation, maintenance, and development of electrical systems, particularly in substation projects and energy infrastructure that require advanced technical skills and strict adherence to safety standards.

Under his leadership, KCA has grown into a company that emphasizes professionalism, quality workmanship, and full compliance with safe work procedures, especially in projects involving Tenaga Nasional Berhad (TNB). His strategic approach and meticulous focus on ensuring efficient operations and a disciplined work culture have been the foundation of the company’s strong reputation in the industry.

TVET Talent Development

KCA plays an active role in developing young national talent by providing structured industrial training specifically designed for TVET graduates. This initiative offers technical students real exposure at project sites, enabling them to learn safe work procedures and understand the engineering standards practiced in the energy industry.

With guidance from highly experienced technical personnel, this program helps shape a new generation of technicians who are more prepared, competent and competitive to meet industry demands.

The combination of technical expertise and strong commitment to human capital development positions KCA as a progressive, trusted and relevant company within Malaysia’s electrical engineering sector.

Strategic Partnership: In collaboration with Kolej Vokasional Seri Manjung under the Ministry of Education Malaysia.
